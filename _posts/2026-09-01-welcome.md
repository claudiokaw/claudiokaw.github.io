I built this site to have one place to think out loud. My days are spent
designing **payments architecture**; my nights and weekends drift toward
**algorithms, optimization, and biology**. Those worlds are less separate than
they look, and this is where I want to explore the seams between them.

Here is roughly what to expect.

**Articles** will be essays and notes — half-formed ideas about network flow,
the economics of settlement systems, or why a slime mold can solve a shortest-path
problem without a brain. **Tutorials** will be hands-on: working code, small
enough to run in one sitting, on machine learning and the algorithms underneath
it. **Projects** and **Competitions** track what I am actually building and
where I test myself.

If any of this is useful to you, that is a bonus. Mostly, writing is how I find
out what I actually think.

Thanks for reading — more soon.
