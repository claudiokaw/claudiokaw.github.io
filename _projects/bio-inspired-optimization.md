## Overview

A study of how biological systems solve optimization problems — and what
algorithms can learn from them. Topics include the slime mold *Physarum
polycephalum* as a natural network optimizer, the geometry of vascular
networks, Flux Balance Analysis in metabolic networks, and ecological networks.

## The core idea

I am interested in the **bidirectional** relationship between biology and
algorithms: biological systems inspire distributed optimization methods, while
mathematical tools illuminate how biological networks self-organize.
