## Overview

As a collaborating researcher at UFABC (Programa Pesquisador Colaborador,
Propes/CMCC), I study **coupled Boolean networks** under the supervision of
Prof. Dr. Luiz C. S. Rozante. Boolean networks are a discrete model of
regulatory and dynamical systems; coupling several of them raises rich questions
about synchronization, attractors, and emergent behavior.

## Why it matters

Boolean networks sit at a crossroads between my interests in **computational
theory** and **biological systems** — they are simple enough to reason about
formally, yet expressive enough to model gene-regulatory and ecological dynamics.
