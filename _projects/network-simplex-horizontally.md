## Overview

An ongoing effort to map the **Network Simplex algorithm** across the many
fields that (re)invent it — logistics, telecommunications, image processing,
and biology. Rather than optimizing one application, the goal is to see the
algorithm *horizontally*: to understand the shared structure of minimum-cost
flow problems wherever they arise.

## Threads

- Classical operations-research formulations of min-cost flow.
- Connections to biological transport networks and self-organization.
